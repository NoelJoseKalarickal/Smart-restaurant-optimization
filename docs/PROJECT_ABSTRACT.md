# Smart Restaurant Supply, Demand & Food Waste Optimization System

The Smart Restaurant Supply, Demand & Food Waste Optimization System is a web-based platform designed to help restaurants efficiently manage ingredients, supplies, orders, and food wastage. The system tracks inventory levels, supplier details, ingredient consumption, incoming orders, and food waste. It analyzes historical demand and sales data to identify frequently used ingredients, predict demand patterns, and support better purchasing decisions. The system provides alerts for low-stock items, overstocking, and products approaching expiry, helping restaurants reduce unnecessary waste and improve resource utilization. It also maintains records of food waste and generates reports to identify major sources of wastage. The platform includes separate roles such as Admin/Manager and Staff, allowing controlled access to relevant operations. Dashboards, search, filtering, and analytical reports help users monitor inventory, demand, purchases, and waste. The system uses a relational database for structured transactional data and a NoSQL database for flexible operational and activity data, providing a practical demonstration of database integration and advanced database operations.
